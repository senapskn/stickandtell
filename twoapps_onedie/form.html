Stick & Tell

Two apps, seven feelings

You will look at screens from two menopause apps, one screen at a time. For each screen, drag the symbol that best matches how it makes you feel onto the screen. A question will appear, and you can write as much or as little as you like. At the end of each app, you can roll a die for a surprise wild card 🎲.

There are no right or wrong answers, and nothing is required. You can skip any question, or any screen. Your honest feelings are what matter. 💬
